How the rounds work

Round 1 Spelling → Round 2 Jumbled Word → Round 3 Fill in the Gaps → Round 4 Super Word. One wrong answer = out (knockout). Rounds 1–3 use the official Level 4 guide words only (442 words, pages 111–133). Super Word uses easy outside-syllabus words in 6 named categories (Science, Cartoon, Sports, Capitals, Environment, Heritage/History) — 2 more categories are unknown and are not invented here.

🎤 Teacher's Stage Guide

Act like the Channel I presenter. Nothing left unprepared — your side and the students' side.

1 · Presenter Script — say it like the show

Opening: “Assalamu Alaikum! Welcome to Spelling Star — Level 4 stage. 100 brave spellers are here today. Only the strongest 10 will reach the Top 10. One wrong answer — and you are out. Spell strong, speak clearly!”

Calling a student: “[Full name], please come to the microphone.” (মাইক্রোফোনের সামনে এসো।)

Spelling round: “Your word is ‘____’. It means ____. Please spell it.” (শব্দটি বানান করে বলো।)

Jumbled Word: “Your letters are: A – R – T – E – H. Arrange them and make the word.” (বর্ণগুলো সাজিয়ে শব্দ বানাও।)

Fill in the Gaps: “Complete the word: _ A _ G E. Fill the missing letters.”

Super Word: “This is a Super Word — outside the syllabus. Your category is Sports. Clue: ____.”

Correct answer: “Correct! Well done, [name] — you stay in the game.”

Knockout — firm but kind: “That is not correct. [Name], you are out of the competition. Thank you — you came bravely.” (ভুল হয়েছে। তুমি এই পর্ব থেকে বাদ। সাহস করে মঞ্চে এসেছো — ধন্যবাদ।)

Top 10 reveal: “And now — our TOP 10! [names]. A big round of applause!”

Top 3: “Three warriors remain! [names].”

Winner: “And the WINNER of Spelling Star Level 4 is — [name]!!”

2 · Stage-Running Checklist

  • Turn order: follow the entry-order list; every alive student answers once per round; survivors advance. Official
  • Flow: 100 students → run rounds until 10 remain → Top 10 → Top 3 → Winner. Round order is fixed: Spelling → Jumbled Word → Fill in the Gaps → Super Word (Super Word cycles repeat until the winner remains). Official
  • Blank answer: give ONE gentle re-prompt — “Please answer.” — then it counts as wrong (knockout). ASSUMPTION — guide does not state this; organizer decision.
  • Mispronunciation / accent: judge the final spelled LETTERS, not the accent. The student must speak each letter clearly, one by one: “B – E – A – U – T – Y”. ASSUMPTION — judge letters, not accent.
  • Timing: keep each turn brisk — about 30 seconds per answer in practice. ASSUMPTION — no official timer stated.
  • Microphone: hold it about 5 cm from the mouth; no tapping, no covering; speak letters loudly.
  • No help: no hints from the audience, no second chances after a wrong answer. Official

3 · Practice-Day Plan

  • Warmup (10 min): 20 easy words spoken aloud, letter by letter.
  • Round drills (30 min): 10 words × each of the 4 rounds, in official order.
  • Mock knockout (40 min): run the full 100 → Top 10 in Tournament mode. One wrong = out.
  • Super Word categories (20 min): rotate Science, Cartoon, Sports, Capitals, Environment, Heritage/History.
  • Daily repeat: Monday–Saturday same plan; Sunday = full mock test. প্র্যাকটিসে বাংলা সাহায্য চলবে, মঞ্চে নয়।

4 · Student Stage Etiquette Card

  • Stand straight and face the judges.
  • Speak clearly into the microphone, letter by letter.
  • Answer only in the required form — spelled aloud, arranged word, or filled word.
  • If knocked out: smile, say “Thank you,” walk off gracefully. (হাসিমুখে “ধন্যবাদ” বলে নেমে এসো।)
  • Do NOT: ask the audience · repeat after the presenter’s hints · argue with the judge · touch the mic stand.
